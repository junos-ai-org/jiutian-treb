# Three-way comparison: T5Gemma v1 vs Gemma-2-2B-IT vs Qwen3-4B — 2026-04-23

## Setup

Third variant added. All three evaluated on the **same 248 stratified
English/TCoT samples** (T5Gemma-tokenizer pre-filtered to ≤4K tokens).

| Variant | Params | Arch | Pretraining base | Backend |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| `t5gemma_v1_2b2b_ul2_it` | 5.6B | enc-dec | Gemma-2 (UL2 adapt + SFT+RLHF+merge) | HF seq2seq |
| `gemma2_2b_it` | 2.6B | dec-only | Gemma-2 (SFT+RLHF+merge) | HF causal |
| `qwen3_4b_instruct` | 4.0B | dec-only | Qwen3 (SFT+RLHF) | HF causal |

Sonnet LLM-judge (parallel subagents) on all 3 × 248 = 744 judgments.

## Overall results

| Variant | Mean score | CORRECT % | +PARTIAL % | TRUNCATED % | WRONG_SELECTION % |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| T5Gemma v1 (5.6B enc-dec) | 4.10 | 32.3% | 42.7% | 17.7% | 25.0% |
| Gemma-2-2B-IT (dec-only) | 3.81 | 30.2% | 43.1% | 12.5% | 22.6% |
| **Qwen3-4B-Instruct** | **4.99** | **33.1%** | 41.9% | **45.6%** | 10.1% |

**Qwen3-4B wins on mean score AND CORRECT%** despite being 43% smaller
than T5Gemma. But it truncates nearly half the time (45.6%) — the 256-token
generation cap is too tight for Qwen3's verbose reasoning style. Giving it
more tokens would likely push CORRECT% even higher.

Qwen3 also has far fewer WRONG_SELECTION (10.1% vs 22-25% for the Gemmas) —
when it DOES commit to an answer, it's much more likely to be right.

## What the 47.6% "all-3-wrong" bucket tells us

118/248 samples where none of these 2-5B models got CORRECT. These cluster
in the hard categories — Table_Selection, Table_Correlation_Analysis,
Table_Column_Naming, Multi-step Data Analysis. Scaling the model (e.g.
Qwen3-14B or 32B) would likely shrink this bucket meaningfully. At the
2-5B tier the benchmark's ceiling is visible.

## Architecture takeaways

Against two decoder-only comparators with different pretraining lineages:

1. **T5Gemma v1's encoder-decoder bump on retrieval holds**: still wins
   Table_Retrieval and Table_Query against Gemma-2. But Qwen3-4B's
   general capability advantage (Qwen3's newer pretraining + Qwen team's
   RLHF) flips the headline.
2. **Enc-dec does NOT provide a clear architecture win** once you compare
   against a stronger dec-only baseline. T5Gemma v1 is pretty much equal
   to Gemma-2-2B-IT (tied headline) but Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 outperforms
   both — this is attributable to pretraining / RLHF quality, not architecture.
3. **Truncation story varies sharply**: Qwen3 > T5Gemma > Gemma-2 in how
   often they run out of tokens. This is a per-model reasoning-style trait
   (Qwen3 loves long chain-of-thought) more than an arch trait.

## Recommended follow-ups

- **Re-run Qwen3-4B with max_new_tokens=1024** — would likely convert a
  big chunk of TRUNCATED → CORRECT and widen its lead. Cheap.
- **Add Qwen3-14B** for the same-pretraining scaling comparison
  (Qwen3-4B → Qwen3-14B → Qwen3-32B). Tells us if the 47.6% all-wrong
  bucket is just a scale ceiling.
- **Relax the 4K-token filter** if the SWA bug doesn't trigger on v1 —
  recovers Multi-step_* tasks that are currently all filtered out.
