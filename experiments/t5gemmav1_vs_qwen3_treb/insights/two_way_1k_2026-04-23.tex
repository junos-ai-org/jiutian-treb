\section*{TL;DR}

On \textbf{1000 stratified TReB English samples}, T5Gemma v1 (encoder-decoder,
5.6B) beats its same-lineage decoder-only counterpart Gemma-2-2B-IT (2.6B) on
overall correctness (\textbf{35.6\,\% vs 30.7\,\%}), mean judge score
(\textbf{4.47 vs 4.02}), and 16 of 26 tasks. The encoder-decoder's advantage is
concentrated in \textbf{retrieval and fact-checking} (+12\,pp and +11\,pp),
consistent with the earlier 250-sample read but now with 4$\times$ the
statistical power and a reproducible, API-based judge.

Encoder-decoder costs \textbf{2$\times$ the parameters} for +5\,pp CORRECT,
and Gemma-2 still wins \textbf{Understanding by +15\,pp}, so architecture
choice is a task-mix question rather than a blanket win. Both models are
wrong on \textbf{55\,\%} of samples --- the 2--5B capacity ceiling reproduces.

\textbf{Two modes track architecture:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Gemma-2 refuses $\sim$4$\times$ more} (3.7 vs 1.0\,\%) ---
        same signal as the 250-run. Same SFT pipeline, so almost certainly
        a decoder-only vs encoder-decoder calibration difference.
  \item \textbf{T5Gemma TRUNCATED fell to 1.9\,\%} (from 17.7\,\% at 250).
        The 250-set over-indexed on long-output Table\_Summary / retrieval;
        stratified 1000 doesn't. T5Gemma is \emph{not} a structurally long
        generator --- max\_new\_tokens=256 is enough.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Reads:}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Retrieval + fact-checking ($n=396$, $\sim$40\,\% of the
        benchmark)} is T5Gemma's strongest advantage. Mechanistically
        consistent with cross-attention --- the decoder re-attends to the
        encoded table at every step, which is the shape of ``find this
        specific thing'' and ``does this claim entail from the table.''
  \item \textbf{Understanding ($n=84$) --- Gemma-2 wins +15\,pp.} These
        tasks reward generic instruction-following and concise rephrasing.
        Mostly reference-free (judged by constraint satisfaction) where
        decoder-only SFT calibration does better.
  \item \textbf{Analysis ($n=104$) --- both at 2.9\,\%.} Distribution
        testing, hypothesis testing, outlier detection, correlation
        analysis all need numeric reasoning neither 2--5B model has.
        Reinforces the 2--5B capacity ceiling.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Take-aways}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Encoder-decoder helps retrieval + fact-checking at 2--5B
        scale.} +12\,pp retrieval, +11\,pp fact-checking. Cross-attention
        is a real architectural lever for table lookup.
  \item \textbf{It's not free --- 2$\times$ params for +5\,pp overall.} If
        your task mix is heavily lookup / entailment / cell-selection,
        T5Gemma pays for itself. For open-ended understanding or math CoT,
        pick Gemma-2.
  \item \textbf{2--5B ceiling is load-bearing.} 55\,\% both-wrong; analysis
        floored at $\sim$3\,\%; multi-step at $\sim$10\,\%. Either scale
        past 4B or design expectations around this.
  \item \textbf{Gemma-2 REFUSAL quirk is not noise.} 3.7\,\% vs 1.0\,\%
        reproduces at larger $n$. Same Google SFT pipeline, so the gap is
        decoder-only calibration --- or UL2 re-cal --- specific.
  \item \textbf{The 250-run TRUNCATED (17.7\,\%) was a sampling artifact.}
        Stratified 1000 gives 1.9\,\%. No reason to raise
        max\_new\_tokens above 256 for T5Gemma v1.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Follow-ups (deferred)}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Gemma-2-9B-IT} as a param-equal comparator (5.6B enc-dec
        vs $\sim$9B dec-only) --- tells us whether the retrieval
        advantage is architecture or capacity.
  \item Probe the SWA-bug threshold on T5Gemma v1; if v1 is bug-free
        we can relax the 4K-token filter and recover the Multi-step tail.
  \item Pull the 37 Gemma-2 refusals and look for a systematic pattern
        (task-type, table length, specific prompt shape).
\end{itemize}
