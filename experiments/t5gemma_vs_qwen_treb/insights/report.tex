\section*{TL;DR}

We set out to compare an encoder--decoder architecture (T5Gemma~2 4B-4B, SFT'd on 100K FLAN) against a decoder-only at matched parameter count (Qwen~2.5 7B Instruct) on the \href{https://huggingface.co/datasets/JT-LM/JIUTIAN-TReB}{TReB} table-reasoning benchmark. The architecture comparison is \textbf{not delivered in this report}. Three reasons:
\vspace{-0.4em}
\begin{enumerate}\setlength\itemsep{0.1em}
  \item A transformers-v5 implementation bug in \texttt{T5Gemma2} caps practical inference at \textbf{4094 input tokens} at \texttt{batch\_size=1} (much less than the model's advertised 128K context). Filed as \href{https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/issues/45521}{huggingface/transformers\#45521}; also filed a feature request for FA2 support as \href{https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/issues/45522}{\#45522}.
  \item Our FLAN-SFT'd adapter \textbf{catastrophically mode-collapsed}: on every probed input it emits a single scalar JSON (\texttt{\{"answer": 10\}}, \texttt{\{"answer": 100\}}, etc.) followed by a repetition loop to \texttt{max\_new\_tokens}. Not a prompt-template issue — happens on plain free-form \texttt{``Q: 2+2? A:''} too.
  \item Base T5Gemma~2 (no SFT) produces \textbf{empty outputs} on most tasks --- expected, since Google only released the pretrained checkpoint.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{-0.4em}
Against that, \textbf{Qwen~2.5 7B Instruct ran the full 7{,}789-sample English split cleanly via vLLM} in $\sim$7 minutes; local metrics landed (EM 0.159, ROUGE-L 0.309, numeric-match 0.434). The infrastructure works; the T5Gemma side of the comparison is the bottleneck.

\section{Setup}

\textbf{Dataset.} \texttt{JT-LM/JIUTIAN-TReB} (7{,}789 English samples across 26 tasks). TCoT (textual chain-of-thought) mode only; PoT and ICoT deferred.

\textbf{Variants.} \texttt{t5gemma\_base} (pretrained), \texttt{t5gemma\_sft} (our LoRA adapter at Hub commit \texttt{abae90a8}), \texttt{qwen\_7b\_instruct} (at native 32K context); a matched-4K Qwen run is planned.

\textbf{Infrastructure.} Custom eval harness (\texttt{eval.py}) with backend dispatch (vLLM for Qwen, HF + optional PEFT merge for T5Gemma). DeepSeek V3 via OpenRouter for LLM-as-Judge (12-class failure-mode taxonomy). Pods on RunPod H100 NVL; auto-termination on completion via per-variant \texttt{.done} sentinels + a background monitor (\texttt{monitor\_pod.sh}).

\textbf{Pipeline hardening (shipped during this session).}
\vspace{-0.4em}
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{0.1em}
  \item \textbf{Incremental writes}: predictions \texttt{flush()}+\texttt{fsync()}'d to \texttt{predictions.partial.jsonl} every 5 batches. Saved 95/100 once after a crash; previously lost all in-flight work.
  \item \textbf{Token-budgeted prompt assembly}: \texttt{build\_prompt\_tcot} encodes head (instruction/title) and tail (question + format instruction) separately, budgets table with remainder. Previously, right-truncation at a 2K cap silently dropped the question on 12\% of samples --- 1-in-8 T5Gemma predictions were literally not answering the question.
  \item \textbf{Adapter-merge safety}: \texttt{merge\_adapter.py} now defaults \texttt{--base} from \texttt{adapter\_config.json:base\_model\_name\_or\_path} instead of a hand-typed model id. \texttt{PeftModel.from\_pretrained} doesn't shape-validate against the original base --- naming mismatches silently produce junk weights.
\end{itemize}

\section{Finding 1: transformers-v5 T5Gemma2 is capped at 4K, not 128K}

Bisected with a targeted 9-sample diagnostic spanning 2.5K--25K tokens at \texttt{batch\_size=1}:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{rcr}
\toprule
\textbf{input tokens} & \textbf{result} & \textbf{tensor~b dim} \\
\midrule
2{,}497 & OK   & --- \\
3{,}525 & OK   & --- \\
5{,}015 & FAIL & 5{,}018 \\
6{,}499 & FAIL & 6{,}502 \\
7{,}493 & FAIL & 7{,}496 \\
9{,}997 & FAIL & 10{,}000 \\
14{,}967 & FAIL & 14{,}970 \\
19{,}808 & FAIL & 19{,}811 \\
25{,}135 & FAIL & 25{,}138 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Every failure raises \texttt{RuntimeError: size of tensor a (4097) must match tensor b (N) at non-singleton dimension 3}, where \texttt{tensor a (4097)} is constant across every failure, and \texttt{tensor b = input\_length + 3}. $4097 = 4 \times \text{sliding\_window}(1024) + 1$ — the signature of a pre-allocated 4-window mask buffer that is not being resized for longer inputs. Affects both \texttt{eager} and \texttt{sdpa} attention paths; \texttt{flash\_attention\_2} is explicitly unsupported for \texttt{T5Gemma2ForConditionalGeneration}. The effective context ceiling is 4{,}094 tokens --- vs.\ the 128K advertised on the \href{https://huggingface.co/google/t5gemma-2-4b-4b}{model card}. Filed upstream.

\section{Finding 2: our FLAN-SFT adapter mode-collapsed}

Five probes on the merged SFT adapter (\texttt{abae90a8}), via an interactive REPL (\texttt{play.py}):

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
\textbf{Prompt style} & \textbf{Task} & \textbf{Gold} & \textbf{Model output} \\
\midrule
TCoT  & Code\_Generation        & Python function       & \texttt{\{"answer": 1\}\{"answer": 2\}\ldots\{"answer": 125\}} \\
TCoT  & Robustness\_Evaluation  & \texttt{neutral}      & \texttt{\{"answer": 10\}} $\to$ loop reproducing the prompt \\
TCoT  & Mathematical\_Reasoning & \$101                 & \texttt{\{"answer": 100\}} $\to$ loop reproducing the prompt \\
Plain & ``Q: What is 2+2? A:'' & 4                     & \texttt{22 + 22 = 44} (repeated $\sim$100$\times$) \\
Plain & word problem, no TCoT   & \$101                 & \texttt{\$1.15 and \$1.10} (repeated $\sim$100$\times$) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Training loss (from the run-1 \texttt{trainer\_state.json} on Hub) went $3.22 \to 1.47$ over 782 steps (batch~128, 100K~FLAN, 1~epoch). Not random — real learning happened — but plateaued higher than our 50-step smoke overfit on 200 samples (loss $0.32$). Combined with the inference behavior, the adapter has a specific failure pattern:
\vspace{-0.4em}
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{0.1em}
  \item Learned the \emph{shape} of FLAN's dominant target (short scalar JSON answer).
  \item Did not learn content: no multi-step reasoning, even on in-distribution arithmetic.
  \item Did not learn to emit EOS: every output runs to \texttt{max\_new\_tokens}, looping on an output it has already produced. Suggests FLAN training targets were ingested without an EOS-termination signal.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{-0.4em}
The \emph{numbers} the model picks carry weak task-linkage (\texttt{100} for a \$101 problem; \texttt{10} for a 3-way classification is random), so the forward pass isn't completely decoupled from input — it's the decoder loop that's collapsed.

\section{Finding 3: the pipeline works for Qwen --- and has upstream knobs to trust}

Qwen~2.5 7B Instruct, full 7{,}789 samples via vLLM at native 32K context, \texttt{batch\_size=8} internal, \texttt{max\_new\_tokens=2048}. Wall-clock: $\sim$7~min on 1$\times$~H100~NVL; cost: $\sim$\$1.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
\textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Overall} & \textbf{Best task} & \textbf{Worst} \\
\midrule
Exact Match     & 0.159 & Table\_Fact\_Checking 0.914 & Instruction\_Following (ref-free) \\
ROUGE-L         & 0.309 & Table\_Fact\_Checking 0.914 & Table\_Column\_Naming 0.028 \\
Numeric Match   & 0.434 & --- & --- \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Raw local metrics undersell Qwen. Per spot-checks, Qwen emits multi-paragraph step-by-step reasoning but doesn't wrap the final answer in the requested \texttt{\{"answer": \ldots\}} envelope --- tasks with EM=0 and ROUGE=0.3-0.5 (Understanding, Title\_Naming, Summary) are format-not-capability failures. The \emph{judge's} structured failure-mode classification (\texttt{FORMAT}, \texttt{PARTIAL}, \texttt{CORRECT}) will disentangle this. Judge run deferred until SFT is replaced.

\section{Where this goes next}

\textbf{T5Gemma side.} Our adapter needs to be redone. Likely improvements, in priority order:
\vspace{-0.4em}
\begin{enumerate}\setlength\itemsep{0.1em}
  \item Audit FLAN tokenization: ensure training targets are wrapped with a proper EOS token. Suspect current training let the model lose its ability to stop.
  \item Use a dataset with longer, multi-step targets (e.g.\ FLAN-CoT, StrategyQA, GSM8K reasoning traces) so the model is tuned to \emph{reason} rather than \emph{emit JSON}.
  \item More epochs --- 1 epoch at this scale likely under-trained; eval loss 1.38 is higher than expected.
  \item Longer input context in training (currently capped at 1024); won't be unlocked for inference until \#45521 is fixed upstream, but doesn't hurt to train for.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Comparison that IS deliverable today.} Even without a usable SFT, we can run \texttt{t5gemma\_base} vs.\ \texttt{qwen@4K} (matched context) on the 100 stratified samples already run. It won't answer ``does SFT close the gap?'', but it will answer ``does the architecture matter before either is instruction-tuned?'' --- a weaker but honest result.

\textbf{Upstream dependency.} Both transformers issues (\#45521, \#45522) need to land before T5Gemma~2's advertised capabilities are reachable via HF. Gemma family precedent (\#37219, \#35290, \#31931, \#41875, vllm\#14881) has been resolved on 1--6 week timelines historically.
